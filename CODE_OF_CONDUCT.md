# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome different backgrounds and
experience levels. Critique evidence and code, avoid personal attacks, and help
others reproduce technical problems. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
sexualized conduct, doxxing and disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

These expectations apply to repository discussions, issues, reviews and project
community spaces. Maintainers may remove abusive content, limit participation
or exclude repeated offenders, with a proportionate explanation when possible.
People affected should not be required to debate their safety publicly.

Until a private reporting route is published, contact a repository maintainer
through an existing private channel if one is available. Do not post another
person's private details publicly. Establish a private conduct-reporting route
before launching a large public community.
